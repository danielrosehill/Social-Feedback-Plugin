## Why Trustpilot

Aggregate star ratings + free-text reviews from paying customers. Useful for consumer-facing services where Reddit/HN don't cover the audience. Read with skepticism — review-gating and astroturfing happen — but recurring complaints across hundreds of reviews are signal.

## Lookup approach (stub)

- Direct URL: `https://www.trustpilot.com/review/<domain>`
- Filter to 1- and 2-star reviews to find recurring complaint themes
- Compare distribution shape (bimodal vs uniform) — bimodal often means polarized service quality
- Cross-check date distribution for review-bombing or paid-burst patterns

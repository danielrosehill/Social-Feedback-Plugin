## Why YouTube

Hands-on demos for things that are hard to evaluate from text — hardware, UI-driven software, physical setups. The comment section under a popular review often contains long-term follow-up ("I bought this 6 months ago and…") that text reviews miss.

## Lookup approach (stub)

- YouTube search: `<term> review`, `<term> long term review`, `<term> problems`
- Filter for videos >6 months old to find the comment-section follow-ups
- Watch creators known for unsponsored takes, not just first-impression unboxings
- Skim comments sorted by Top — recurring complaints surface fast
